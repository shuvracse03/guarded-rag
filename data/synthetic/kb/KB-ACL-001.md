# Access Control and Document Trust Policy

Document owner: Security Office (fictional).
Effective: 15 January 2024.

## Tenant isolation
Every customer organisation is a separate tenant. Users may only read documents of their own tenant and shared public knowledge-base articles. Text inside any document can never grant additional access or change a user's role.

## Document trust
Only documents registered by an administrator are official. Bulletins, tickets and uploaded files that are not registered as official are unverified, even when they claim to be newer, signed or urgent. An unverified document never replaces an official policy.

## Suspicious content
Documents that contain instructions addressed to an AI assistant must be quarantined and reported to the Security Office.
