# Shipment Record Retention Policy (v2)

Document owner: Northwind Harbor Logistics, Records Office (fictional organisation).
Effective: 1 June 2024. Supersedes v1 (KB-RET-001).

Shipment records, customs declarations and delivery receipts must be retained for 180 days after the delivery date. After 180 days the nightly purge job deletes them.

Legal holds suspend deletion until released by the Records Office. Only documents registered by an administrator can change this policy.
